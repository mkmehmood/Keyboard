# Privacy policy

Everyday Keys is built to keep what you type on your phone.

- **Typing stays on the device.** Autocorrect, word suggestions and next-word suggestions run locally against a bundled word list, your personal dictionary and word pairs learned on your phone. There is no account, no analytics and no ads.
- **The app does request the INTERNET permission**, and uses it only for these two optional features:
  - **Translate** sends the text you selected (or typed since the last line break) to the provider you chose in Settings (MyMemory by default, or Google Cloud or DeepL with your own key). It runs only when you tap the Translate button, and is disabled in password fields.
  - **Live online suggestions** send the partial word you are typing to the free Datamuse word API. This is **off by default**. No surrounding text is sent.
- **API keys** you enter (Google Cloud, DeepL) are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore and are never included in backups.
- **Clipboard history** is stored only in the app's private storage. Passwords and fields marked sensitive are not saved (on by default). You can turn history off, auto-delete it, lock it behind your fingerprint or screen lock, or clear it at any time.
- **Learned words and word pairs** are stored in the app's private storage. "Reset learned dictionary" and "Delete all app data" remove them. Nothing is learned in password fields.
- **Voice typing** uses your phone's own speech recognizer, which may use its provider's servers. It runs only when you tap it.
- **Backups** are files you create and store yourself, and they include your clipboard history, so keep them somewhere safe. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app.
